U.S. refund information

Know the difference between a refund and compensation.

For flights to, from or within the United States, U.S. Department of Transportation rules can require refunds in certain circumstances, including cancelled flights and significant changes when the passenger does not accept the alternative travel arrangement.

  • Cancelled flight where the passenger does not continue with the booked travel
  • Significant schedule or itinerary changes where the passenger does not accept the changed travel
  • Qualifying refunds for certain paid ancillary services that were not provided
  • Keep evidence of the original booking, disruption notice and relevant expenses

Frequently asked questions

Important information before submitting your case.

Does every Ryanair cancellation qualify for compensation?

No. A refund, reimbursement and compensation are different concepts, and eligibility can depend on the route, timing, cause of the disruption and applicable law. Ryanair itself states that compensation may be available in some cancellation circumstances.

What should I enter as my claimed amount?

Enter the amount you are seeking or believe may be due, based on your ticket/refund amount and relevant qualifying expenses. This is not a guarantee that the amount will be awarded.

Why do you ask for my home address?

The address can be used as part of the case record and client contact information. Please provide an accurate address so your claim record is complete.

What happens after I submit?

The form prepares a structured WhatsApp message containing your submitted claim details and opens WhatsApp to the support helpline. The support team can then review the case manually.

What documents are most useful?

Usually the booking confirmation, original and revised itinerary, cancellation or delay notices, airline correspondence, receipts for relevant expenses and any existing case or complaint reference.

What if I accepted a rebooked flight?

Accepting and travelling on an alternative flight can affect whether a ticket refund is available under U.S. DOT refund rules. The details of what you accepted and what changed should be reviewed before a claim is submitted.

How long can a refund take?

For refunds covered by U.S. DOT rules, the Department says refunds generally must be provided within 7 business days for credit-card purchases and 20 calendar days for other payment methods once the refund is due. Actual processing can depend on the airline or ticket agent and the circumstances.
